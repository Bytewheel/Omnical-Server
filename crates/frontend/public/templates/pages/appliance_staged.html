What happens next

  1. The service restarts.
  2. The staged database replaces the live one, with no process holding it open.
  3. Pending migrations are applied.
  4. Row counts are compared against the archive's manifest, and the result is on the appliance page when you log back in.

The comparison is against the archive, not against what was there before. A restore deliberately moves data back in time, so every row written since the archive was taken is meant to disappear — that is not damage, and the report will not present it as any.
