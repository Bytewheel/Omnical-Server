{{ user.id }}'s share links

A share link is a secret URL anyone can subscribe to (Apple "Add Subscribed Calendar", Google "From URL", any webcal client) — no account needed. Anyone with the URL can read the collection; revoke to make it stop working immediately.

For a calendar, you can also send a one-time registration invite: the invitee joins the group and gets full add/delete/modify/read access to its shared collections. Use "Generate invite link" to get a shareable link without needing their email.

You can also subscribe a guest — an external person without a portal account — to one of your calendars. After creating the invite you will see their account + app token once; send them the server URL, the username and the token, and they add the calendar in any CalDAV client. Revoke to stop their access immediately.
